Bluetooth isn't available here

This page needs Chrome on an Android phone (Bluetooth switched on).

iPhone: use Stagio Wi-Fi instead. On the Stagio PC: Remote → No Wi-Fi, or the phone can't reach this PC? → Stagio Wi-Fi → Start, then scan the two codes. With Stagio on a Mac: turn on the phone's hotspot and join it from the Mac (the steps are under Remote).

中文：此页面需要 Android 手机上的 Chrome。iPhone 请改用 Stagio Wi-Fi（电脑上点 Remote → No Wi-Fi… → Stagio Wi-Fi → Start，再扫两个二维码）。Mac 上的 Stagio：打开手机热点，让 Mac 连上（步骤在 Remote 里）。

Connect to the Stagio computer

  1. On the computer (Windows or Mac): Remote → No Wi-Fi…? → Bluetooth → Start.
  2. Enter the 6-digit PIN shown there, tap Connect, and choose the computer in the list.

中文：先在电脑上 Remote → No Wi-Fi… → Bluetooth → Start；在这里输入电脑上显示的 PIN，点 Connect，选择那台电脑。
